# ICP — who to sell ShopMate to

## Ideal customer profile

**Small-to-medium online store or brand owners** who are already selling
somewhere (Shopify, Instagram/Facebook shop, WhatsApp catalog, a basic
website) but handle customer conversations manually or not at all.

Concretely, look for stores with:

- **1,000–20,000 followers/subscribers** on the platform they sell through.
  Fewer than ~1,000 and there usually isn't enough order volume to justify
  a monthly fee yet; more than ~20,000 and they likely already have a
  dedicated support team or an existing chatbot vendor.
- **Visible signs of manual or absent customer support**: slow reply times
  in their Instagram comments/DMs, no visible FAQ/chatbot, customers asking
  the same shipping/sizing/returns questions repeatedly in public comments.
- **Repeatable, catalog-style products** — apparel, accessories, beauty,
  home goods, food/supplements, digital products. (Highly custom
  one-off/bespoke products, like commissioned art, are a worse fit — the
  catalog+order flow assumes mostly-fixed pricing.)
- **English-speaking market** for the first wave (US/UK/EU/AU store owners)
  — better ability to pay in USD, and cold email in English is where the
  agency's existing experience is strongest. CIS/Uzbek-market stores are a
  credible second wave (ShopMate already ships English+Russian).
- **Already spending money on tools** — a paid Shopify plan, paid ads, an
  existing paid chatbot (ManyChat, Chatfuel) — signals they already believe
  in paying for growth infrastructure, which makes the sale easier.

## Where signs of "cart abandonment problem" show up

- Comments like "sent you a DM, no reply" or "does this come in size M?"
  under posts, unanswered for days
- A "link in bio" that goes to a slow-loading Linktree with no chatbot
- Reviews mentioning slow shipping communication or no order tracking
- Visible seasonal sales spikes (holidays, drops) where they'd clearly
  struggle to keep up with DMs manually

## Who to avoid (bad fit)

- Stores under ~500 followers (likely under $500-1000/mo revenue — a $19+/mo
  tool is a hard sell)
- B2B / wholesale-only sellers (this product is built for consumer,
  chat-based ordering)
- Stores that already run a mature chatbot stack (ManyChat + Zapier +
  Shopify flows) — you'd be replacing, not adding, which is a much longer
  sales cycle
- Anything requiring real-time inventory sync with a POS system (ShopMate's
  catalog is admin-managed, not auto-synced — see `FUTURE_IDEAS.md`)

## Why ShopMate is priced the way it is (vs. general chatbot platforms)

Generic platforms like ManyChat or Chatfuel charge **$15-45/mo** for
audience-size-based messaging tools that are *not* store-specific — the
store owner still has to build their own product catalog flow, FAQ logic,
and abandoned-cart automation inside those tools themselves (which most
never actually finish doing).

ShopMate is priced similarly ($19-39/mo) but ships the *entire* store flow
already built — catalog, order collection, FAQ, cart-abandonment
reminders, broadcasts — plus a one-time setup fee that includes actually
loading their real catalog and connecting a payment method. The pitch is
"a finished employee, not a tool you still have to configure yourself."
