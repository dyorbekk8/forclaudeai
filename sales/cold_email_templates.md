# Cold email templates

Three emails: an opener, a follow-up (3-4 days later, only if no reply),
and a final low-pressure note (7 days later). Plain text, no images, no
links in the first email (better deliverability, and it reads more like a
real person than a campaign).

Fill in the bracketed `[...]` fields per lead before sending. Keep them
short — these are meant to be read on a phone in 15 seconds.

---

## Email 1 — the opener

**Subject:** quick question about [Store Name]'s DMs

```
Hi [First Name],

I was looking at [Store Name]'s Instagram and noticed a few comments
asking about sizing/shipping that took a while to get answered — totally
normal at your size, just something I help stores fix.

I build a Telegram bot that handles this for you automatically: customers
browse your products, order, and get FAQ answers without you lifting a
finger, and you get a message the second someone orders. If someone looks
at a product and doesn't buy, it follows up for you a couple hours later.

Would it be worth a 5-minute look at a live demo? I can send you a link to
try it yourself, no call needed.

[Your Name]
[Your Agency Name]
```

---

## Email 2 — follow-up (send 3-4 days later, only if no reply)

**Subject:** re: quick question about [Store Name]'s DMs

```
Hi [First Name],

Following up in case this got buried — no worries if now isn't the right
time.

Quick version: it's a Telegram bot for your store. Customers can browse
products and order right inside a chat, it answers common questions on its
own, and it nudges anyone who looked but didn't buy. You get an alert the
moment an order comes in.

Setup takes me a day, and I can show you a working demo built with a few
of your actual products before you decide anything.

Worth a quick look?

[Your Name]
```

---

## Email 3 — final, low-pressure (send 7 days after email 1)

**Subject:** last note from me — [Store Name]

```
Hi [First Name],

I'll stop following up after this one — didn't want to be another email in
your inbox.

If handling customer questions and orders automatically ever becomes a
priority, feel free to reach out any time — happy to send over a demo, no
pressure either way.

Wishing [Store Name] a great [month/season]!

[Your Name]
```

---

## Notes on tone (why these are written this way)

- No urgency tricks ("last chance", "today only") — these read as genuine,
  specific outreach from someone who actually looked at the store.
- No links in email 1 — a cold email with a link is more likely to get
  filtered as spam and reads less personal. The ask is a reply, not a click.
- Each one is short enough to read on a phone without scrolling.
- Personalize the noticed-detail line in email 1 for real — a generic
  "I noticed you could use more sales" without a specific detail reads as
  templated and hurts reply rates.
