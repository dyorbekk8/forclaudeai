## Description
Every lie someone tells you makes you 1% taller.

People tell about 1.65 lies a day. 53 lies and you're taller than any human in history. 866... and you're taller than Mount Everest.

Then the internet finds out, and everyone you love starts lying to you to make you feel better. The only way back down is the truth.

Who always tells YOU the truth? Tag them in the comments.

Is any of this real?
- Height = 160 cm × 1.01^lies: ×53 ≈ 2.72 m (Robert Wadlow, tallest person on record), ×408 ≈ 93 m (Statue of Liberty with pedestal), ×628 ≈ 828 m (Burj Khalifa), ×866 ≈ 8,849 m (Mount Everest).
- Serota et al. (2010) found people self-report about 1.65 lies per day on average. Above 8,000 m climbers call it the "death zone" because of low oxygen.
Leo, Mia and the lie rule are fiction. Narration: synthetic voice.
