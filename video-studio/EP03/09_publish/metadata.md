## Description
A basketball hits you in the face... and THEN you see it coming.

Starting today, your life has lag. Everything you see, hear and feel reaches you late, and every day the lag doubles. One second. Sixteen seconds. Eight minutes, as old as sunlight. A whole year.

So what happens when you start seeing the past... and the past includes someone you lost?

Would you take the lag if it meant one more day with someone you lost? Tell me in the comments.
