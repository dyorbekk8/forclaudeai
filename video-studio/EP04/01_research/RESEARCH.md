## 2. Findings
1. **Length: 7–11 minutes** is the sweet spot for every top hit. Our EP01 and EP02 were 4–5 minutes.
   Longer videos mean more watch time per impression.
2. **The two hottest engines are time/speed** (4.7M, 2.2M, 2.1M, 895K, 687K) and **runaway escalation, "...and Couldn't Stop"**
   (2.2M, 875K, 370K, 374K, plus 100K+ for many brand-new small channels: The Readout, What If Sim, Off The Scale).
3. **"See everyone's X" is now flooded.** For the same title you find 5–10 channels at 0–100 views.
   Only channels that already have momentum win there. This is why our "secret / betrayal / lie" videos got 0.
4. Almost every obvious idea gets copied within days. **Being early on an idea beats having a better copy.**

## 3. The gap we found
"If people lagged in real life": **33.6M + 10.6M** (Daniel LaBelle), **5.0M** (CalebCity), 869K (Darius Dinero).
Huge proven demand for the *lag* concept, all of it live-action comedy.
In the animated 2nd-person "What If" niche the concept is **almost untouched** (one video, 15 views).
Combine it with the hottest title engine:

**→ "What If You Started Lagging and Couldn't Stop?"**

- Time/perception (hot engine #1) + runaway escalation (hot engine #2) + gamer word "lag" (huge YT audience).
- Built-in numeric ladder: lag doubles daily: 1 s → 8.5 min (= sunlight) → 36 h → 24 days → 1 year.
- Real science beats: the brain processes vision about 0.1 s late; Moon light takes 1.3 s; sunlight takes 8 min 20 s.
- Emotional core (what sets top videos apart): seeing the past means you see the dog you lost, and your sister leaves notes for the "future you".
- Visuals that look good when drawn in code: PING HUD, buffering spinner, RGB split, onion-skin "ghost" trails (the past world).

## 4. Production rules for EP03 (from bible + this data)
- 8–9 min, about 1,150 words, narrator about 90%, short character lines.
- Hook in the first 5 s: the effect before the explanation. Restate the rule by 0:30.
- A level card on every jump ("DAY 7 · 64 s"), with the HUD always on screen = progress bar = retention.
- Never a static frame for more than 2 s; a new visual idea every 3–4 s.
- ElevenLabs v3 voice (Brian) with audio tags for emotion.
- Thumbnail: character with an RGB glitch ghost, red "999 ms" PING tag, 2 words max.
