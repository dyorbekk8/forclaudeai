# Ifverse craft notes (vidIQ watch of "Everyone Has a Power Level. Mine Is 1.", 64K views in 1 day, plus thumbnails)

Structure: effect-first hook (0–10 s) → oppressive low point → first stand → rule card → doubling escalation montage → moral setback (power halves) → crucible → boss climax where the hero ACTS → twist recontextualises the power as care → cosmic cliffhanger.
Camera: ASL 2.5–3.5 s (<1.5 s in action, 4–6 s in reflection). Low-angle wides for power imbalance, OTS for confrontations, ECU on eyes/fists, top-down god's-eye shots, slow push-ins on resolve, whip/crash zooms on impacts, 2.39:1 letterbox for premium feel.
Light/colour: weakness = cold desaturated blues/greens; growth = sunset orange/dusk purple; boss = neon + deep shadow + red accents. Warm rim light on black silhouettes, volumetric lamp cones, light pools, DOF blur, dust motes, smoke.
Acting: eyes do everything (drooping lids = shame, angled brows = resolve, pinprick pupils = fear, crescents = warmth); slouch/pockets = powerless, upright/chin up = defiant; idle breathing always.
Sound: melancholy piano → ticking/percussion pulse → brass/epic strings. Total silence before the biggest hit, sub-bass drops on level-ups, crisp foley, environmental ambience. Narrator: low, intimate, micro-pauses before numbers.
UI: glowing pill badges above heads (gold = ally, red = hostile), top-left HUD, sliding rank ladder.
Thumbnails: dark scene, one strong light source, rim-lit black head, eye glint/sparkle, smug half-lidded eyes, 2 words (white + yellow), no sticker outlines.
