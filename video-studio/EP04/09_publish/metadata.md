## Description
Every time someone says your name, you get 1% faster.

Sounds small. But 62 people make you as fast as Usain Bolt. 396 and you break the sound barrier. 1,771... and you're racing light itself.

Then your video goes viral. And Einstein's relativity starts stealing time from everyone you love.

Who would say your name every day? Tell me in the comments.

Is any of this real?
- 1.01^n compounding: 24 km/h × 1.01^62 ≈ 44 km/h (Usain Bolt's top speed ≈ 44.7 km/h); × 1.01^396 ≈ 1,235 km/h (speed of sound); × 1.01^747 ≈ 40,600 km/h (Earth's escape velocity ≈ 40,270 km/h); × 1.01^1771 ≈ the speed of light.
- Special relativity: at 99.99% of light speed, time runs about 70× slower for you than for people at rest.
Leo, Mia and the name rule are fiction. Narration: synthetic voice.
