Is any of this real?
- Blink n moves you 10^(n-1) metres: blink 6 = 100 km (the Kármán line, where space begins), blink 10 = 1 million km (past the Moon, 384,400 km), blink 17 ≈ 1 light year, blink 22 ≈ 105,000 light years (wider than the Milky Way), blink 28 ≈ 105 billion light years (beyond the ~46 billion ly radius of the observable universe).
- People blink roughly 15–20 times per minute. Voyager 1 launched in 1977. Proxima Centauri is 4.24 light years away. Andromeda holds about a trillion stars.
