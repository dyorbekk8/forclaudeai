Is any of this real?
- IQ = 100 × 1.01^insults: ×34 ≈ 140 (often called "genius" range), ×70 ≈ 200, ×231 ≈ 1,000. IQ tests are normed to an average of 100; scores far above ~200 aren't meaningfully measurable.
