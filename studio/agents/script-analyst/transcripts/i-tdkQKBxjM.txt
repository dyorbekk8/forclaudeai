[00:20] It changes nothing about the legal state of war between the parties.
[00:23] It suspends the shooting and nothing else, and either side can resume by giving notice or by giving none.

[01:35] Capitulation, the surrender of a specific force or a defended position agreed between military commanders rather than governments.
[01:45] It ends the fighting for those troops in that place and has no effect on the war itself.

[03:48] Peace accord.
