**B — cold open = the strangest item from the END of the list:**

Formula: (1) dated specific anomaly → (2) contradiction with what viewers assume → (3) scope + count → (4) open loop + 2nd-person commitment.
**Thumbnail alignment:** in all three matched B videos the cold-open subject is the thumbnail's red "?"/stamp tile.
**Rule:** cold open ≤25 s from the last/strangest item, explicit loop + count; first item by 0:25.

## 3. Structure
1. (B) cold open.
2. Optional 15 s "how this list works" (U82kw@00:54 "…goes from the most common to the rarest. One of these countries no longer exists.").
3. Items in a declared escalating order: ranks low→high; severity weakest→most final; size crew→theater; commonness common→rarest; colour sets default member first.
4. **Last item = system-breaker / meta item** (11/13 A videos): Need to know (y1cfJ@07:32), Task Force (3pJGG@07:45), Broselow (QlPLt@09:12), "White is also where the system runs out" (KgHM7@13:09). Ladders end on the absurd top rung (Washington, Dewey).
5. Closing reframe that calls back item 1.

## 5. Pacing
- WPM median **156** (122–183; A 155, B 166). Words/sentence median 13. Sentences ≤5 words: 13%.
- Seconds per item: 19–25 (28–30 items), 30–40 (14–16 items), 50–75 (8–12 items).
- Top-10 runtime 485–833 s. First word at 0.0–1.1 s.
- DEFCON (5 items, 3:26) flopped at 7.8K.
**Rule:** 155–165 wpm, 8–12 min, ≥8 items.

## 6. Retention devices
1. Paradox line in every item.
2. Escalation + best-for-last, announced.
3. Planted callback: EyN93@00:44 "$2,200 a month… worth remembering for later" → @07:36 "eight and a half times what you made at the bottom".
4. Dated micro-stories with human stakes.
5. Myth-busting (uikZS@01:04; .50-cal myth KgHM7@11:41).
6. 2nd-person immersion (B).
7. Mid-CTA at 39–55%, between items, ≤6 s ("If you're finding this useful so far, a like and a subscribe help the channel more than anything else." QlPLt@05:02). Counter-example: gimmick CTA at 10% inside item 1 (0tQDh@01:05) — 14K vs the channel's 133K.

## 7. Ending
A: reframe → "Thanks for watching. <viewer-placing question>? Drop it in the comments…" 8–14 s ("What rank did you make?", "What color are the fire hydrants on your street?").
B: ends on the payoff, 0 s outro.
**Rule:** end on the reframe; outro ≤8 s with a self-placing question.

## Script template
```
TITLE: Every <System> Explained | How <System> Actually Works
ITEMS: N (8–30), ORDER: lowest→highest | most common→rarest | default→strangest
TARGET: 155–165 wpm, 8–12 min
[COLD OPEN 15–25 s] (the thumbnail's red-tile item)
  On <date>, <institution> <did something contradicting expectations>.
  <Contradiction with what viewers assume.>
  "There are <N> <items> in this system, from <bottom> to <top>."
  "The last one <paradox teaser> — and it comes last."   (B) "You start at the bottom."
[ITEM k]
  <LABEL>. <GIST ≤8 w>. <MECHANISM + number>. <SPECIFICS>. <TWIST>.
  <STORY: On <date> in <place>, <event + human cost>> (long items). <BUTTON ≤14 w>.
  (B ladders) one sentence describing exactly what is on screen.
[MID CTA 45–55 %, between items, ≤6 s]
[LAST ITEM] system-breaker / absurd top rung = cold-open payoff
[CLOSE] reframe calling back item 1 / cold open; optional ≤8 s self-placing question.
[SOURCES] every number/date → sources.md
```

## Do not
- No channel intro, logo sting, "In this video", "Hey guys"; first word ≤1 s.
- No transition filler; cut straight to the label.
- No CTA in the first 40% or inside an item; no gimmick CTAs.
- No lists <8 items, videos <6 min, or sequels.
- No item without a hard number and a button.
- No jokes/slang; irony from facts only. Don't switch register mid-video.
- Avoid low-stakes workplace/infrastructure taxonomies.
- No unsourced facts — one wrong number in the comments kills trust.
