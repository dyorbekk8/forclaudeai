The F-35 should have been called the F-24. Every letter and number in a U.S. military aircraft name follows a system that dates to 1962 — basic mission, modified mission, vehicle type, status prefix, design number and series letter — plus the rule-breakers like the SR-71, F-117, B-21 and F/A-18, and how missile names like AIM-9 and RGM-84D work.
