Every letter in a U.S. military aircraft name, one by one — from F for fighter (and why the F-35 should have been the F-24) to Q for unmanned, V for VIP and Y for prototype.

CHAPTERS
0:00 F — Fighter
0:25 A — Attack
0:37 B — Bomber
0:53 C — Cargo
1:14 K — Tanker
1:38 E — Electronic
1:54 H — Rescue · Helicopter
2:13 M — Multi-Mission
2:27 U — Utility
2:42 R — Reconnaissance
3:00 W — Weather
3:11 V — VIP · Vertical
3:32 L — Cold Weather
3:41 Q — Unmanned
3:52 P — Patrol
4:06 S — Anti-Submarine
4:22 T — Trainer
4:30 O — Observation
4:43 X — Experimental
5:00 Y — Prototype
5:09 G — Grounded
5:18 J — Special Test
5:29 Numbers and the last letter

Based on the 1962 Tri-Service aircraft designation system (now maintained under DoDI 4120.15 / DAFI 16-401 / AR 70-50 / NAVAIRINST 13100.16).
